Brand & Business Challenge

Client & Industry Background

Founded in 2019 in Kuching, Malaysia, Radiant Health began as a single clinic with a singular vision. Starting as a single clinic, it has expanded to eight locations across East and West Malaysia, reflecting its commitment to accessible, comprehensive care.

Radiant Health integrates cutting edge AI technology with modern medical practices and traditional healing methods, emphasizing preventive care, mental well-being, and personalized treatment plans. Its services include integrative medicine, nutritional counseling, and wellness programs, catering to diverse patient needs.

Studio SOLD's Role

This rapid expansion presented a unique challenge: evolving the brand from a local service provider into a cohesive national ecosystem without losing the intimate, personalized care that defined its origins.

The new brand identity needs to both reflect the brand's core values, belief system and also to represent cutting-edge technology to reflect the clinic group's ambitions.

Bridging Paradigms

The brand stands at a complex intersection of cutting-edge innovation and traditional care. The challenge was to integrate 'High Tech' AI diagnostics with grounded "Asian Value" symbolism with 'High Touch' traditional healing methods into a unified visual narrative. We needed to avoid the cold sterility often associated with medical tech while maintaining the professional authority required of healthcare professionals and leaders.

Combating Category Saturation

The Malaysian healthcare landscape is saturated with predictable blue-and-white visual tropes. To carve out a defensible market position, we needed to disrupt these category conventions. The challenge was to create a visual vernacular that signaled 'innovation' without alienating a conservative demographic, ensuring the brand stood out on the shelf of consumer choice as a modern, forward-thinking alternative.

Design Process

Our strategic foundation was built on Proverbs 4:23: 'as you unwrap my words, they will impart true life and radiant health into the very core of your being.' This scripture inspired the core brand philosophy of 'Imparting True Life,' guiding every visual decision to reflect vitality, restoration, and inner wellbeing.

Visual Synthesis

We explored deep Asian symbolism to find resonance with the brand's heritage. The 'Bamboo' was selected for its representation of Asian resilience, flexibility, and upward growth, mirroring the patient's journey to recovery. Paired with the 'Radiant Sun,' a universal symbol of energy symbolizing positivity in the name.

Color & Craft

Departing from the standard clinical blues of the industry, we developed a earthy-green-neon palette that feels both medical and trustworthy yet warm and inviting while maintaining a modern vibe. The design system balances precise grid-based layouts (representing technology) with organic textures and fluid lines (representing biology and healing).

Typographic Vernacular

To articulate the brand's dual nature, we curated a typographic pairing that balances function with feeling. We selected a geometric sans-serif to carry the heavy lifting of wayfinding and medical data, paired with a humanist display typeface for headlines that injects warmth and empathy. This distinctive typographic voice ensures that even purely functional communications feel 'human-first'.

Design Solution

The delivered solution is not just a logo, but a responsive & dynamic identity system. The responsive logomark adapts its complexity based on context, from large-scale signage to favicon constraints, ensuring high fidelity and brand recall across all channels. We coupled this with a humanist typographic system that balances legibility with approachability, establishing a typographic hierarchy that guides users intuitively through complex medical information.

Omnichannel Experience Design

Beyond identity, we orchestrated a cohesive Total Experience (TX). We translated the brand system into physical space (wayfinding, interior ambiance) and digital touchpoints (patient portals, responsive web design). The result is a seamless user journey where every interaction, from booking appointment online or stepping into a clinic, reinforces a unified, trusted, and premium brand voice.

Digital Innovation & Interface

Translating the new identity into the digital realm, we designed a patient-centric web experience that prioritizes accessibility and user flow. The UI/UX design utilizes the atomic design methodology, ensuring scalability. The result is a digital platform that not only informs but builds trust, seamlessly integrating appointment bookings, telemedicine and health records into a intuitive, reassuring interface.
