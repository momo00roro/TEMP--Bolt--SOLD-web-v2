## Brand & Business Challenge
## Client & Industry Background
 KOBA is a visionary concept brand born from the legacy of Kobayashi Woven (小林商标), a multinational textile leader established in Kyoto in 1947. Deriving its name from &quot;Kobayashi&quot;—where &quot;Koba&quot; (小林) translates to &quot;Small Forest&quot;—the brand embraces its origins in the mountainous forests of Kyoto.
 This intrinsic connection to nature drives KOBA's mission: to weave eco-sustainable solutions into the very fabric of the industry. By respecting its roots, KOBA embodies a deep reverence for the mountains and forests, affirming its drive for a greener future without compromising on quality.
## Studio SOLD's Role
 The challenge was to craft a sophisticated brand identity that transcends borders, positioning KOBA as the vanguard of premium eco-textiles. We needed to create a visual narrative that creates a seamless dialogue between the meticulous artistry of traditional Japanese craftsmanship and a forward-thinking commitment to environmental stewardship.
 This required a delicate balance—avoiding the common tropes of generic &quot;green&quot; branding while ensuring the message of sustainability was unmistakable, premium, and deeply rooted in culture.
## Heritage & Artistry
 Drawing from over 75 years of expertise, the brand honors the meticulous attention to detail found in traditional Kyoto textile making. We highlighted this heritage by integrating subtle, nature-inspired motifs and a refined, minimalist aesthetic that speaks to the quiet dignity of Japanese design.
## Sustainable Innovation
 Beyond aesthetics, the brand identity signals a radical shift towards sustainability. We emphasized KOBA's dedication to the planet through a &quot;forest-first&quot; philosophy, showcasing how cutting-edge technology can coexist with nature to produce fabrics that are both luxurious and responsible.
 [IMG https://cdn.sanity.io/images/bsz0zxd1/production/4edf064b3035a59574bbfde0724950aed0efe795-1920x1280.webp] 
## Design Process
 The strategy centered on the duality of &quot;Craft and Future.&quot; The process began with extensive research into Japanese fabric and textile industries, traditional Japanese weaving motifs and the technical evolution of sustainable fabrics and fibers.
 Studio SOLD shaped the brand direction, visual identity, digital interface, and supporting art direction into a cohesive system. The design balances tactile references to textile craft with a more modern digital presentation.
## Discovery
 This discovery phase informed a strategic positioning that defines KOBA as both a guardian of heritage and a pioneer of innovation. The team explored minimalist visual languages, natural color palettes, and tactile art direction to ensure the brand translates seamlessly into digital spaces.
## Visual Mapping
 This involved mapping out a visual system that could scale across diverse touchpoints—from high-end art direction to a refined UI/UX experience. This exercise allows the team to visually project the brand’s values and identity into different touchpoints across physical and digital spaces

## Brand & Business Challenge
## Client & Industry Background
 Founded in 2019 in Kuching, Malaysia, Radiant Health began as a single clinic with a singular vision. Starting as a single clinic, it has expanded to eight locations across East and West Malaysia, reflecting its commitment to accessible, comprehensive care.
 Radiant Health integrates cutting edge AI technology with modern medical practices and traditional healing methods, emphasizing preventive care, mental well-being, and personalized treatment plans. Its services include integrative medicine, nutritional counseling, and wellness programs, catering to diverse patient needs.
## Studio SOLD's Role
 This rapid expansion presented a unique challenge: evolving the brand from a local service provider into a cohesive national ecosystem without losing the intimate, personalized care that defined its origins.
 The new brand identity needs to both reflect the brand's core values, belief system and also to represent cutting-edge technology to reflect the clinic group's ambitions.
## Bridging Paradigms
 The brand stands at a complex intersection of cutting-edge innovation and traditional care. The challenge was to integrate 'High Tech' AI diagnostics with grounded &quot;Asian Value&quot; symbolism with 'High Touch' traditional healing methods into a unified visual narrative. We needed to avoid the cold sterility often associated with medical tech while maintaining the professional authority required of healthcare professionals and leaders.
## Combating Category Saturation
 The Malaysian healthcare landscape is saturated with predictable blue-and-white visual tropes. To carve out a defensible market position, we needed to disrupt these category conventions. The challenge was to create a visual vernacular that signaled 'innovation' without alienating a conservative demographic, ensuring the brand stood out on the shelf of consumer choice as a modern, forward-thinking alternative.
 [IMG https://cdn.sanity.io/images/bsz0zxd1/production/36a6ae2d0bc4be3b736ac29b8aa8194529f75f3e-1400x933.webp] 
## Design Process
 Our strategic foundation was built on Proverbs 4:23: 'as you unwrap my words, they will impart true life and radiant health into the very core of your being.' This scripture inspired the core brand philosophy of 'Imparting True Life,' guiding every visual decision to reflect vitality, restoration, and inner wellbeing.
## Visual Synthesis
 We explored deep Asian symbolism to find resonance with the brand's heritage. The 'Bamboo' was selected for its representation of Asian resilience, flexibility, and upward growth, mirroring the patient's journey to recovery. Paired with the 'Radiant Sun,' a universal symbol of energy symbolizing positivity in the name.
## Color & Craft
 Departing from the standard clinical blues of the industry, we developed a earthy-green-neon palette that feels both medical and trustworthy yet warm and inviting while maintaining a modern vibe. The design system balances precise grid-based layouts (representing technology) with organic textures and fluid lines (representing biology and healing).
## Typographic Vernacular
 To articulate the brand's dual nature, we curated a typographic pairing that balances function with feeling. We selected a geometric sans-serif to carry the heavy lifting of wayfinding and medical data, paired with a humanist display typeface for headlines that injects warmth and empathy. This distinctive typographic voice ensures that even purely functional communications feel 'human-first'.

## Design Solution
 The delivered solution is not just a logo, but a responsive & dynamic identity system. The responsive logomark adapts its complexity based on context, from large-scale signage to favicon constraints, ensuring high fidelity and brand recall across all channels. We coupled this with a humanist typographic system that balances legibility with approachability, establishing a typographic hierarchy that guides users intuitively through complex medical information.
## Omnichannel Experience Design
 Beyond identity, we orchestrated a cohesive Total Experience (TX). We translated the brand system into physical space (wayfinding, interior ambiance) and digital touchpoints (patient portals, responsive web design). The result is a seamless user journey where every interaction, from booking appointment online or stepping into a clinic, reinforces a unified, trusted, and premium brand voice.
## Digital Innovation & Interface
 Translating the new identity into the digital realm, we designed a patient-centric web experience that prioritizes accessibility and user flow. The UI/UX design utilizes the atomic design methodology, ensuring scalability. The result is a digital platform that not only informs but builds trust, seamlessly integrating appointment bookings, telemedicine and health records into a intuitive, reassuring interface.

## Brand & Business Challenge
## Client & Industry Background
 Listed on the mainboard of SGX since 2012, Cordlife is one of the leading providers of cord blood and cord lining banking services in Asia. Cordlife’s market presence spans across six key markets of Singapore, Hong Kong, Indonesia, India, Malaysia and Philippines. Cordlife has recently expanded via strategic interests in Thailand, Vietnam and Myanmar..
 Looking to establish a stronger foothold in the mother and child segment, Cordlife launched Moms Up, an every-stage motherhood resource mobile app. Inspired by Cordlife’s vision to help people live healthier, happier, and longer through innovation and technological advancement, Moms Up launched in the six key markets.
## Studio SOLD's Role
 Launching a unified motherhood platform across six distinct Asian markets (Singapore, Hong Kong, Indonesia, India, Malaysia, Philippines) presented a complex localization challenge. We needed to distill a universal 'motherhood' experience that resonated deeply with diverse cultural sensibilities while maintaining a singular, cohesive brand voice for Cordlife.
 Studio SOLD were engaged by Cordlife's Moms Up team to shape the brand, from forming the Brand Strategy, developing the Brand Identity, crafting the Brand Applications, Designing the User Experience and User Interface of the mobile app, to Designing the Social Media assets.
## Client Background

## Studio SOLD's Role
 Launching a unified motherhood platform across six distinct Asian markets (Singapore, Hong Kong, Indonesia, India, Malaysia, Philippines) presented a complex localization challenge. We needed to distill a universal 'motherhood' experience that resonated deeply with diverse cultural sensibilities while maintaining a singular, cohesive brand voice for Cordlife.
## Destigmatizing Support
 In a digital landscape often cluttered with medical jargon or superficial advice, the challenge was to position Moms Up as a trusted, empathetic companion. The goal was to break down the barriers to seeking help, transforming a utility app into an emotional anchor that validates the struggles and joys of modern motherhood.
## Unified Ecosystem
 Cordlife's expansion into the B2C segment required a pivot from a clinical service provider to a daily lifestyle partner. We faced the architectural challenge of integrating complex resource libraries, tracking tools, and community features into a seamless mobile experience that felt intuitive for sleep-deprived parents.

## Design Process
## User-Centric Prototyping
 Studio SOLD adopted a human-centered design approach to bridge the gap between medical utility and emotional support. By mapping the motherhood journey, we identified key friction points and opportunities for delight.
## Empathetic Discovery
 Our strategy began with deep-dive 'Brand Discovery' sessions, mapping the emotional peaks and troughs of the motherhood journey. We developed detailed user personas and empathy maps through hybrid surveys and interviews, ensuring our design decisions were rooted in real human needs rather than assumptions.
## Playful Semiotics
 Moving away from sterile corporate identities, we developed 'Stylescapes' focused on 'Approachable, Playful, and Authentic' aesthetics. We utilized a semiotic language of 'Overlap Hearts' (bonding) and 'Balloons' (uplifting) to create a visual system that feels like a warm embrace—optimistic, comforting, and inherently positive.
## User-Centric Prototyping
 The UI/UX phase involved rigorous iteration. From low-fidelity wireframes to high-fidelity prototypes, every interaction was tested for friction. We focused on one-handed navigability (essential for nursing moms) and glanceable data visualization, ensuring the app remains functional even in the most chaotic parenting moments.

## A Scalable Visual System
 We delivered a responsive brand identity system capable of scaling from an app icon to billboard campaigns. The visual language uses a proprietary illustration style that transcends language barriers, allowing the brand to speak clearly to mothers in Mumbai, Jakarta, and Singapore alike.
## Hub of Empowerment
 The final Moms Up app is more than a tool; it’s a digital sanctuary. By organizing vast resources into intuitive 'stages' of motherhood, we created a resource hub that grows with the user. The architecture prioritizes personalized content delivery, ensuring relevant advice is always at the user's fingertips.

 The unified design system streamlined the rollout across six diverse regions, reducing development overhead while maximizing brand consistency. The result is a regionally recognized platform that has successfully repositioned Cordlife as a holistic partner in the parenting journey, driving significant uptake in user acquisition and engagement.

## Brand & Business Challenge

It is imperative for us to simplify complex auction mechanics into a frictionless, linear digital journey that is intuitive and friendly. The goal was to democratize the bidding process, making it accessible and reassuring for non-technical users looking to sell their cars quickly.

## Brand & Business Challenge
## Client & Industry Background
 Singapore Chinese Chamber of Commerce and Industry is a pillar in shaping the commerce landscape in Singapore since 1906. It plays a key and pro-active role in representing the interests of the local business community. The SCCCI has over 5,000 corporate members and has more than 160 trade association members (representing over 40,000 companies from a wide spectrum of trades and industries) in Singapore.
## Studio SOLD's Role
 In 2026, it celebrates its 120th year with its running theme: 双甲子聚商, which means 120 years of connecting and gathering commerce.
 The challenge was to create an anniversary identity that honored this monumental legacy without feeling archaic. We needed a symbol that bridged the rich history of the past with a dynamic vision for the future.
## Visual Unification
 With a diverse membership ranging from traditional family businesses to modern tech startups, the new identity had to resonate across generations. The mark needed to be authoritative yet progressive, unifying these disparate groups under a single, celebratory banner that signaled strength, continuity, and shared prosperity.
## Anniversary Significance
 This wasn’t just a rebrand; it was a commemorative campaign for a specific milestone. The design challenge was to craft a &quot;120&quot; mark that could stand alone as a powerful graphic element for the anniversary year, yet integrate seamlessly with the existing SCCCI parent brand, enhancing rather than overshadowing it.

## Design Process
## Heritage Analysis
 Our process began with a deep dive into the chamber's archives, analyzing 120 years of brand evolution. This historical audit provided the foundation for a design that is rooted in heritage but interpreted through a modern lens.
## Heritage Analysis
 We studied the architectural motifs of the SCCCI building and the typographic styles of past decades. We extracted key visual DNA—specifically the bold strokes of Chinese calligraphy and the structural integrity of the chamber's emblem—using them as the building blocks for the new identity.
## Symbology of Growth
 The numerals &quot;120&quot; were crafted to intertwine, symbolizing the unity and interconnectedness of the business community. The upward trajectory of the strokes represents continuous growth and the chamber's role in elevating Singaporean businesses on the global stage. The &quot;0&quot; is designed to resemble a globe, hinting at international expansion.
## Color Psychology
 We retained the traditional 'Imperial Red' to command authority and respect, honoring the chamber's roots. To mark the celebration, we introduced a 'Jubilee Gold' accent. This pairing creates a festive yet dignified palette that balances the joy of the occasion with the gravitas of the institution.
