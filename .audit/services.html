Our Expertise

Six Core Services

Studio SOLD is a design studio in Singapore, working AI-first. AI supports the process behind our client work, from traceable research to brand knowledge, review, and delivery. Our team leads strategy, creative direction, and final work.

See how these capabilities come together in our branding and digital design case studies, or start a conversation about your project.

AI in our process

Four systems we are building

Project research assistant

Bring briefs, stakeholder interviews and desk research into one view. AI can group recurring themes, surface unanswered questions and link insights to their sources, helping our team build a stronger strategy from evidence.

Brand knowledge system

Make approved positioning, messaging, guidelines and project decisions searchable in one place. Designers can bring the right brand context into a website, campaign or new identity touchpoint.

Brief-aware review assistant

Check selected deliverables against the approved brief and brand guidance. The system can flag missing requirements or inconsistent language for our designers to assess before presentation or handover.

Production and handover workflows

Turn approved content, asset lists and decisions into organised checklists and handover documents. Automating repeated preparation can make the next steps clearer for clients and their teams.

These are systems we are developing around the creative process. They support our designers and client teams. They do not replace the people creating the brand identity, website or digital experience.

How We Work

Our 12-Step Branding Process

Every project begins with a conversation to understand your goals, context, audience, and practical requirements before we recommend the right approach.

STEP 01 Onboarding

Kick Off / Intro Call

Our entire process is built on trust, so before we take on any new project, we kick things off with a quick call.

STEP 02 Onboarding

Proposal

Once we understand what you're looking for, we estimate out a project budget and timeline for you to review.

STEP 03 Onboarding

Contract & Deposit

Once our project gets the green light, we'll sign a contract and collect a deposit. Typically, we ask for 50% of the project fee up front.

STEP 04 Onboarding

Setup Client Portal

Upon receipt of the initial deposit payment, we will set up a Client Hub via Notion or Google Drive.

STEP 05 Strategy

Discovery / Strategy Call

We kick every project off with a candid, casual get-to-know-you process. Deliverable: Strategy Document.

STEP 06 Strategy

Direction / Moodboards

Before we launch full-steam into creative work, we put together Moodboards with photos, illustrations, colors, and fonts.

STEP 07 Design

Identity Design

After ONE Moodboard approval, design work begins. An initial brand identity usually takes about four weeks.

STEP 08 Presentation

Design Presentation

A typical SOLD branding project includes 2 identity directions with brand foundations, positioning, and personality.

STEP 09 Presentation

Feedback & Revisions

A standard project scope includes two rounds of revisions. Live with it, know thy audience, and involve stakeholders.

STEP 10 Launch

Sign Off & Balance

Once we have signoff approvals for all deliverables, we will collect the remaining balance.

STEP 11 Launch

Brand Rollout

Once we received the final payment, it's time to share it with the world. Logos, fonts, artwork, style sheets—all yours.

STEP 12 Offboarding

Offboarding Call

An offboarding call is scheduled at the end of the branding project to ensure you have everything you need.
